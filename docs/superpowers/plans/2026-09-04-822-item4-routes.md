# #822 — boolean intersection, uv planar, deform sine, sent to Maya for the first time

Redmine #822, fourth item of the #818 audit: three value-selected routes no
gate had ever sent over the wire. Probes (kept): `evals/item4_probe_822.py`
(A), `_822b.py` (B); findings under `evals/item4_probe_822/`. Maya 2027,
agent 9878, repo cwd. All three routes had a defect.

## boolean_op op=intersection

Works where it should: two overlapping unit cubes give a 0.5-cube (volume
0.125, 12 tris, watertight), a's pivot and a's material carried, both
operands consumed, no leftover nodes.

**An empty result was reported as success.** Disjoint cubes, cubes sharing
exactly one face, and a `difference` whose b covers a all return a mesh with
0 faces and 0 vertices — `tris: 0`, **`watertight: true`** (0 boundary
edges), `uv_bounds: null`, no warning — and both operands are gone. A
`union` of disjoint cubes is fine (two shells, volume 2).

Fix: `intersection` pre-checks the operands' world bboxes
(`sculpt_math.bbox_intersection`; touching is not overlapping) and refuses
before the checkpoint is spent, operands untouched. Every op post-checks the
result's face count in `_do_boolean`: an empty mesh is deleted and the call
refused, naming the auto-checkpoint to restore, because the operands cannot
be brought back any other way.

Not this ticket: `listSets` reports a ghost `initialShadingGroup` on every
boolean result while the shape's `instObjGroups` is connected to `redSG`
alone (measured on both ops; pre-existing).

## uv_atlas project=planar

`polyProjection md="z"` is **world** −z whatever the mesh faces (a
transform-rotated plane confirmed world space): a sheet facing X or Y got
16/16 faces at zero UV area, a cube 4/6, a sphere its far 200/400 faces
mirrored over the near ones — all `inside_patch: true`, no warning. Maya's
`md="b"` ("best plane") gave the standing sheet 0 collapsed faces in the GUI
Maya and **16/16 under mayapy** — not a flag to build on.

Fix: `uvmath.thinnest_axis` picks x/y/z from the mesh's world bbox (a tie —
a cube — keeps z, the old default); `uvmath.face_uv_stats` measures every
face's signed UV area after the projection and `warnings` names, per mesh,
the zero-area faces and the **minority-winding** faces. Minority, measured:
a sheet projected from its back has every face wound the other way, a
whole-mesh flip with nothing stacked; the sphere's far hemisphere is the
real case. `pack_shape` returns `warnings`; `uv_atlas` lifts them to the
result's own `warnings` prefixed with the mesh; `assemble` reads only
`inside_patch` and is unaffected.

## deform deformer=sine (and wave)

`cmds.nonLinear` scales its handle to **half the mesh's largest extent** and
positions it at the bbox centre (2-tall cylinder → 1, 10-tall → 5, 4×1×1
slab → 2, 1×6×2 box → 3). sine reads amplitude, wavelength and offset in
handle units: amplitude 0.2 moved the 2-tall cylinder 0.2 and the 10-tall
one 1.0. wave the same (0.062 → 0.969). The inert hint said "amplitude is in
scene units, not a ratio" — false until now. Bake was clean, handle
rotate/translate work, the inert warning fires, all six attrs are plain
`double`.

Fix: `sculpt.HANDLE_LENGTH_PARAMS` — sine's amplitude/wavelength/offset and
wave's plus minRadius/maxRadius are divided by the handle's scale before
`setAttr`, so the number means scene units on any mesh. Bounds stay
handle-local (Maya's convention, as for flare in assemble). Now amplitude
0.2 moves both cylinders 0.2; wave on the 10-tall one 0.062.
